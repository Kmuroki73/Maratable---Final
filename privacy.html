Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared with MaraTable Group.

Contact enquiries

When you complete the contact form on this website and select “Send enquiry”, your device opens your default email application with your message addressed to info@maratablegroup.com. You must review and send that email yourself. The website does not submit or store the form contents.

Information you provide

The prepared email may include your name, email address, company (if provided), enquiry type and message. Please do not include sensitive personal information or information you are not authorised to share.

How we use enquiries

If you choose to send the email, MaraTable Group uses the information to review and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to follow up on a potential supply, customer, supplier or investment relationship.

Your email provider

Once you send the message, its transmission and handling are also subject to the terms and privacy practices of the email service you use. The website cannot send the message on your behalf.

Contact

For questions about privacy or the information you have emailed to us, contact info@maratablegroup.com.

This page describes the current contact-form process. It should be reviewed and supplemented if the website’s data collection or processing practices change.
