Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we handle information shared with MaraTable Group.

Contact enquiries

When you submit the contact form, the details you provide are securely sent to MaraTable Group through the website contact service. The configured email provider (SMTP2GO or Resend) delivers the enquiry to the team’s designated inboxes. Your email address is included as the reply-to address so our team can respond to you.

Information you provide

The prepared email may include your name, email address, company (if provided), enquiry type and message. Please do not include sensitive personal information or information you are not authorised to share.

How we use enquiries

If you choose to send the email, MaraTable Group uses the information to review and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to follow up on a potential supply, customer, supplier or investment relationship.

Service providers

The configured email provider (SMTP2GO or Resend) processes enquiry emails on our behalf. Cloudflare Turnstile provides bot protection for the contact form. When you submit the form, the verification token and your IP address are sent to Cloudflare’s Siteverify service to validate the request before the enquiry is accepted. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Cookies and local storage

The website displays a cookie preference notice. Your choice to accept or reject optional cookies is saved in your browser using local storage so the notice does not appear on every visit. Essential site preferences, such as your selected display language, may also be stored locally. Optional analytics will only run if configured and you have accepted optional cookies.

Contact

For questions about privacy or the information you have emailed to us, contact info@maratablegroup.com.

This page describes the current contact-form process. It should be reviewed and supplemented if the website’s data collection or processing practices change.
