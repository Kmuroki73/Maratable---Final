MaraTable Group · Healthy Kids

Growing healthier,
happier futures.

Children's wellbeing is shaped by the food they eat and the healthy habits they build. Healthy Kids celebrates nourishing choices, positive routines and everyday food awareness that help children grow, play and thrive.

Why Healthy Kids matters

Healthy habits start early.

Children benefit when nutritious choices are familiar, varied and enjoyable. Through Healthy Kids, MaraTable Group encourages positive food habits, balanced meals and a better understanding of the role wholesome foods play in everyday wellbeing.

Our focus is practical, positive and rooted in the foods that nourish growing families.

Our focus

Small choices. Lifelong benefits.

Healthy Kids celebrates practical ways to support children's wellbeing through nourishing food, positive routines and everyday food awareness.

01
Nutrition awareness

Help children discover a variety of nourishing foods and understand the value of balanced meals.

02
Wholesome food

Celebrate fruits, vegetables, grains and other wholesome foods as part of varied everyday meals.

03
Healthy everyday habits

Encourage families and communities to make healthy food choices familiar, accessible and enjoyable.

Everyday nourishment

Helping healthy habits grow.

From colourful fruits and vegetables to grains and other natural foods, variety makes meals interesting and helps children build a positive relationship with food.

Healthy Kids is about encouraging families and communities to make nourishing choices part of everyday life.
